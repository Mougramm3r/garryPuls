Eigene Sounds für PULSE
=======================

Jeder Sound hat hier einen eigenen Ordner. Leg beliebig viele .wav/.mp3/.ogg hinein,
dann wird jedes Mal zufällig eine Datei gespielt. Ordner leer = Standard-Sound
(Sounds ohne Standard bleiben stumm, bis du eine Datei hineinlegst).

Tipps:
- Dateinamen nur Kleinbuchstaben, keine Umlaute, keine Leerzeichen.
- Kurze Dateien (unter 1 MB); Freunde laden sie beim Joinen automatisch herunter.
- .wav am besten 44100 Hz, 16 Bit, mono für Sounds im Raum.
- Neue Dateien werden nach einem Map-Neustart erkannt.
- Anhören: Entwickler-Menü (Shift + Rechtsklick auf den Tab SERVER) → Sounds.
- Gruselsounds direkt in diesem Ordner (alte Variante) funktionieren weiterhin.

Ordner:
Hunter:
  behindu/behind/          Behind You: behind the victim
  behindu/turn/            Behind You: victim turns around
  hunter/roar/             Roar
  hunter/teleport/         Teleport
  hunter/chaser/           Chaser Pulse (only the hunter)
  hunter/jumpscare/        Jump Scare (the victim hears it)
  hunter/blackout/         Blackout
  hunter/doorslam/         Door Slam
  hunter/trap/             Trap snaps shut
  hunter/trap_place/       Trap placed
  hunter/stalk/            Stalk starts (only the hunter)
  hunter/mark/             Marked (the victim hears it)
  hunter/mimic/            Mimic starts
  hunter/nightvision/      Night Vision on (only the hunter)
  hunter/ping/             Noise Radar ping (only the hunter)
  heartbeat/               Heartbeat (sensor and victims)
  ui/toggle/               Ability on / off

Victim:
  victim/flash/            Flashlight Blind
  victim/blinded/          Hunter is blinded (the hunter hears it)
  victim/silent/           Stay Silent
  victim/decoy/            Decoy thrown
  victim/decoy_land/       Decoy lands
  victim/adrenaline/       Adrenaline
  victim/exhausted/        Out of stamina
  victim/hidden/           Hiding bonus active

Items:
  items/pickup/            Item picked up
  items/pills/             Calming Pills
  items/glowstick/         Glowstick
  items/camera/            Camera Flash

Sanity:
  sanity/whispers/         Hallucination whispers

Round:
  round/prep/              Hiding phase starts
  round/hunt/              The hunt begins
  round/final/             Final phase begins
  round/caught/            Victim caught / died
  round/win_hunter/        Hunter wins
  round/win_victims/       Victims win
  ui/click/                Menu click

Atmosphere:
  music/                   Chase music (final phase, last minute)
  ambient/                 Ambient loop (louder over the round)
  stingers/                Random short scares every 25-60 s

Gruselsounds (Soundboard des Jägers):
  scary/                   Scary Sounds for the hunter's sound board (every file = own entry)
